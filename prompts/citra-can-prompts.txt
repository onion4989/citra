CITRA can image prompts
=======================
Purpose: Website product overlays matching citrus splash hero video
Count: 5 variants
Updated: camera lock + fresher green citrus direction + transparent BG

------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMON (prepend to every variant)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Photoreal product packshot of ONE slim 330ml aluminum beverage can.

CAMERA LOCK (must be identical across all 5 images):
- Locked-off tripod camera, eye-level
- Exact same distance, focal length feel (approx. 85mm product lens)
- Exact same three-quarter angle: can rotated ~25 degrees to the right
- Exact same framing: can centered vertically, full can visible with equal margins
- Same can scale in frame (can height fills ~70% of image height)
- No camera move, no zoom difference, no tilt difference between variants
- Straight horizon, no Dutch angle

CAN GEOMETRY (must be identical):
- Same slim can proportions, same lid, same pull-tab orientation
- Same grounding shadow style (soft contact shadow only)
- No cropped edges

BACKGROUND:
- Fully transparent background (PNG with alpha)
- No floor, no seamless paper, no water scene, no props behind the can
- Isolated product only

BRAND / STYLE:
- Fictional brand wordmark: "CITRA"
- Fresh, juicy, modern premium sparkling citrus drink
- Green citrus direction (lime–green citrus / green mandarin feel), NOT yellow lemon
- Avoid cheap clipart, avoid cluttered stickers, avoid comic outlines
- Clean contemporary label: generous whitespace OR clear color fields, refined typography
- Condensation / mist allowed only on the can metal, subtle
- No people, no extra logos, no watermark, no Japanese text
- Soft daylight product lighting, crisp metal reflections

OUTPUT:
- Single can, transparent background, high resolution, sharp label type

VARIANT 1 — Green Grove Fresh
------------------------------------------------------------------------

[COMMON]

Label concept: Green Grove Fresh.
- Soft matte foam-cream can body
- Large "CITRA" wordmark in deep leaf green

Keep design elegant, not busy.
